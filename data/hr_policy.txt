HR Policy Document

Notice Period Policy:
Employees must serve a notice period of 60 days before resignation. Early release requires HR and manager approval.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees may request work from home for valid reasons such as health issues, family emergencies, or project requirements. Manager approval is required.

Maternity Leave Policy:
Eligible female employees are entitled to maternity leave as per company policy and applicable government regulations. HR approval is required.

Employee Conduct:
Employees must follow workplace discipline, confidentiality, attendance, and code of conduct requirements.
