Leave Policy Document

Casual Leave:
Employees are allowed 12 casual leaves per year.

Sick Leave:
Employees can apply for sick leave. Medical proof may be required if leave exceeds two consecutive days.

Leave Approval:
All leave requests are first marked as pending. The manager or HR team can approve or reject leave requests.

Leave Cancellation:
Employees can cancel only pending leave requests. Approved or rejected requests cannot be cancelled by employees.
